🧐 What is gesture-synth?

gesture-synth is an amazing musical instrument that turns your hand movements into sound. Instead of pressing keys or strumming strings, you simply move your hands in front of your computer's camera, and beautiful chords play. It's like having an invisible piano in the air!

Imagine waving your left hand to set a chord, and moving your right hand up or down to change the melody. It's intuitive, magical, and perfect for beginners who have never played music, as well as experienced musicians looking for a completely new way to express themselves.

The app watches your hands using advanced camera technology, instantly recognizing your gestures and turning them into rich, layered sounds. You don't need any prior training - just launch the program and start experimenting with your hand movements.

⚙️ How gesture-synth Works

When you start gesture-synth, it activates your camera. The app's smart tracking system detects your hand position, the angle of your fingers, and the distance of your hands from the screen. These movements are instantly translated into musical chords and notes.

Two hands play different roles your left hand controls the chord (harmony), and your right hand layers the melody on top. Move both hands together, and you create a full, rich musical piece. The speed of your movement adds natural dynamics, just like playing a real instrument.

💡 Tips for the Best Experience

  • 💡 Lighting: Sit in a well-lit room so your hands are clearly visible to the camera. Avoid sitting with your back to a bright window, as that casts a shadow on your hands.
  • 🕺 Distance: Keep yourself about 2-3 feet (60-90 cm) from the camera so your full hand movements are captured.
  • 🙌 Background: Choose a plain, uncluttered background to help the camera focus solely on your hands.
  • 🎵 Experiment: Try slow, wide movements versus quick, small movements. Different gestures create different moods in the music.

🔧 Having Trouble?

Camera not working?

Make sure no other application (like Zoom, Teams, or your photo app) is using your camera right now. Close those programs and restart gesture-synth. Also, check your computer's privacy settings to make sure you've allowed app access to your camera.

Sounds are distorted or cutting out?

Check your system volume and your speaker volume. If it sounds too loud, simply lower the volume. For best audio quality, use speakers rather than laptop built-in audio, but both will work perfectly fine.

My hands are not being detected properly?

Ensure your hands are entirely visible in the camera frame. Try to avoid wearing long sleeves that cover your wrists, and take off any gloves. Good lighting is the number one fix for this issue.
