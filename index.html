About Me

I am a final-year Biotechnology student at BRAC University, Bangladesh, with a strong focus on computational biology, machine learning, and statistical modelling. My research bridges the gap between high-dimensional biological data and interpretable predictive models.

My research spans computational biology, statistical modelling, and machine learning, with work published in venues including IEEE and Springer. I have contributed to projects on Bayesian inference of cancer evolution, antimicrobial resistance prediction, multi-omics patient stratification, and AI-assisted drug discovery.

I am currently preparing for a Master's in Quantitative Finance at Bayes Business School, with the goal of applying advanced statistical and machine learning methods to financial markets, risk modelling, and quantitative trading.

Beyond research, I serve as a peer reviewer for Briefings in Bioinformatics (Oxford University Press) and was selected for the GCI World 2026 AI & Data Science Programme at The University of Tokyo.

Research Experience

Bayesian Beta-Mixture Model for Clonal Evolution Inference

IEEE BECITHCON 2026

Tools: Python, PyMC, NumPy, Matplotlib, SciPy

Developed a hierarchical Bayesian Beta-Mixture model to infer clonal evolution patterns from high-dimensional DNA methylation data in haematological cancers. Implemented MCMC sampling (NUTS) for posterior inference and validated against simulated datasets.

Independent Validation of ML Model for AMR Prediction

Springer CCIS · SEMIT 2026

Tools: Python, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Pandas, SHAP

Built and independently validated a machine learning framework for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) risk prediction using multi-omics data from 18,916 bacterial isolates. Identified key genomic features driving resistance through SHAP-based interpretability analysis.

DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-39711-9_24

ML-Based Patient Stratification in EGFR-Mutant Lung Adenocarcinoma

Springer CCIS · SEMIT 2026

Tools: Python, Scikit-learn, PCA, t-SNE, UMAP

Implemented an integrative multi-omics machine learning framework combining genomics, transcriptomics, and epigenomics data to stratify patients with EGFR-mutant lung adenocarcinoma. Identified distinct molecular subtypes with differential survival outcomes.

DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-39711-9_23

Drug Repositioning Hub — AI-Assisted Platform

Independent Project

Tools: Python, Streamlit, Scikit-learn, NIH Drug Repositioning Hub API

Developed a computational platform leveraging AI and the NIH Drug Repositioning Hub to identify novel therapeutic uses for existing drugs. The platform integrates molecular similarity analysis, pathway enrichment, and ML-based repurposing predictions.
